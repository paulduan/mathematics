
平行线分线段成比例亦称平行截割定理，平面几何术语，指三条平行线截两条直线，所得的四条线段对应成比例，如图l1∥l2∥l3_，_则AB:BC=DE:EF.

```latexsvg
\documentclass{standalone}
\usepackage{tikz}
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}
    % 定义三条平行线
    \draw[thick] (-2,3) node[above] {$l_1$} -- (6,3);
    \draw[thick] (-2,2) node[above] {$l_2$} -- (6,2);
    \draw[thick] (-2,0) node[above] {$l_3$} -- (6,0);
    % 定义两条斜线
    \draw[domain=-2:1.8] plot ({\x},{\x+1}); 
    \draw[domain=5:4] plot ({\x},{-4*\x+20}); 

% 标记交点
    \coordinate (A) at (2,3);
    \coordinate (D) at (4.25,3);
    \coordinate (B) at (1,2);
    \coordinate (E) at (4.5,2);
    \coordinate (C) at (-1,0);
    \coordinate (F) at (5,0);
% 标记点
    \foreach \point in {A,B,C,D,E,F}
        \fill (\point) circle (1.5pt);
  % 标注点的名称
    \node[above] at (A) {$A$};
    \node[above] at (D) {$D$};
    \node[left] at (B) {$B$};
    \node[right] at (E) {$E$};
    \node[left] at (C) {$C$};
    \node[right] at (F) {$F$};

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```
