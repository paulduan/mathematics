$$z=a+bi$$
$$z=P(r,\theta)$$
$$r=\left\lvert z\right\lvert$$ r是模长度，$\theta$是幅角   
$$P(r,\theta)=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$$
如果r=1
$$P(1,\theta)=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$$

复数相乘
$$P(r_{A},\theta_{A}) \times P(r_{B},\theta_{B})=P(r_{A}r_{B},\theta_{A}\theta_{B})$$
